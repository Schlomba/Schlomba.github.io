About Us

Founded on a sunny windowsill in 2019, the Leafy Business Team started as a single fern with big ambitions. Since then, we've grown (pun intended) into a full roster of plants dedicated to office morale, air quality, and the occasional dramatic wilt for attention.

We believe every workspace deserves a little greenery, a lot of personality, and a consistent watering schedule. Our team is rooted in collaboration, growth, and mutual respect for each other's sunlight needs.

Meet the Team
